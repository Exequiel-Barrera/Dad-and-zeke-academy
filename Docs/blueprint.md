# Dad & Zeke Academy Blueprint

## Vision

Dad & Zeke Academy is a tablet-first educational platform created to help Zeke improve reading, writing, maths, confidence, independence and persistence through story-driven learning adventures.

## Main User

Zeke, age 6, Year 2.

## Parent User

Dad can guide learning, track progress, choose missions and celebrate achievements.

## Learning Worlds

### Reading Forest
Focus: reading fluency, comprehension, vocabulary, confidence.

### Writer's Workshop
Focus: handwriting, spelling, capital letters, full stops, short sentences.

### Maths Mountain
Focus: counting by 2s, 5s, 10s, place value, addition and subtraction to 100.

### Discovery Lab
Focus: science, curiosity, experiments and questions.

### Kindness Kingdom
Focus: confidence, persistence, mistakes, kindness and independence.

### Hall of Fame
Focus: stars, badges, trophies, certificates and progress.
